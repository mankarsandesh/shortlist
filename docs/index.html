Get started in a minute

Shortlist is free for everyone. There’s no limit on contacts or templates, and nothing to sign up for.

  1. Add Shortlist to Chrome

    Install it from the Chrome Web Store (coming soon). It works in Chrome 116 or later on desktop.

  2. Save a profile

    Open any LinkedIn profile and click Save to Shortlist in the bottom-right corner.

  3. Open your pipeline

    Click the Shortlist icon in your toolbar. Your contacts open in the side panel.

  4. Reach out

    Pick a template, click Copy & mark contacted, and paste the message into LinkedIn.

Common questions

Is Shortlist really free?

Yes. Every feature is free for everyone, including unlimited contacts, unlimited templates and CSV export. There’s no account, no trial and nothing to pay.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, using Chrome’s storage on your computer. Shortlist has no servers of its own, so your contacts, notes and templates never reach us.

Does Shortlist automate LinkedIn?

No. It reads the profile you’re looking at, and only when you click Save. It never sends messages, visits profiles or saves people in bulk. You copy a message and paste it into LinkedIn yourself.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Your contacts are stored in one Chrome profile and don’t sync between computers. To take them with you, export a CSV from Settings.

What happens if I uninstall it?

Chrome deletes everything Shortlist stored. To keep a copy, export a CSV from Settings first.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome on desktop, version 116 or later. Shortlist uses Chrome’s side panel, which isn’t available on mobile.

Is Shortlist made by LinkedIn?

No. Shortlist is an independent tool and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
